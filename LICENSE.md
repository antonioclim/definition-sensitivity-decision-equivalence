## How to locate the scope

`RIGHTS_INVENTORY.json` maps the actual outer files and every member of `scientific/ODSA_SCIENTIFIC_RECORDS_PC1.zip` to the scope categories. `RIGHTS_SCOPE.json` records the approved grants and exceptions. `MANIFEST_SHA256.json` binds the actual outer bytes; the unchanged inner manifest binds the scientific archive's members.

The MIT grant covers the author's own Python, JavaScript, HTML application source, tests, schema implementations and reproduction/dependency configuration. It includes the recovered legacy source and the four recovered independent FAT components only to the extent they are the author's own work; the public review record is documentation rather than code. The seven `.do` files in `scientific_records/frozen_simulation/08_R3_VERIFICATION/official_world_bank_source/` are excluded from this MIT grant and retain the original World Bank notice.

CC BY 4.0 covers the author's own software and protocol documentation, original drawing/source/export contributions, scientific audit narratives and approved aggregate result contributions. It does not assert exclusive rights over individual numbers, source facts or material outside the author's rights. Where an own result or figure uses provider facts, the grant on the author's presentation does not replace the source terms.

## Exclusions and priority

The raw OpenAlex files under `oa/02_OA_DATA_AND_RESULTS/batch_01/raw/` and `attempts/` retain their recorded provider terms. Provider facts or fragments in acquisition, coverage, candidate/normalised-input and provenance records are excluded from a new blanket author grant. Rights in indexed full article texts are not conveyed by the metadata.

All applicable World Bank source notices are retained verbatim. Neither the source-code notice nor these author grants license the controlled FAT microdata, observation identifiers or protected working reports. Those inputs and reports are absent from this distribution. The four newer FAT numerical blocks, their eligible roster and exact eligible count remain withheld. The factual World Bank source-route record does not grant rights to the catalogue page or dataset it identifies.

The manuscript and supplementary manuscript prose are maintained separately and are excluded from this software distribution and these software-distribution grants.

A specific upstream notice or exclusion governs its identified material. Listing MIT and CC BY 4.0 does not make the whole archive dual-licensed at the user's choice. Historical R19/PC1 receipts describing licences as proposed or unassigned remain unchanged as evidence of their earlier preparation state. The present scope records the author's later approval; it does not rewrite that history.
